Privacy Policy

Whistle IQ — last updated September 27, 2026

Whistle IQ does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

What the app stores, and where

Whistle IQ has no account system, no login, and no server backend. Everything the app remembers about your usage — quiz progress, your missed-question bank, streaks, badges, and any diagrams you save in the Court Diagram Builder — is stored only in your device's local browser storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is never shared with any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data permanently deletes it.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or any third-party service that collects data about you or your device.

Network access

The app loads its own fonts from Google Fonts on first load (a standard web font request, which does not include any personal information about you). Aside from that, the app works fully offline and makes no other network requests.

Children's privacy

Whistle IQ does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect data — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
